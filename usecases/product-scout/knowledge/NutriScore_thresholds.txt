Nutri-Score Thresholds Cheatsheet
=================================

General foods (excluding cheeses, fats/oils, and beverages):
- A: -15 to -1 points (most favorable)
- B: 0 to 2 points
- C: 3 to 10 points
- D: 11 to 18 points
- E: 19 points and above (least favorable)

Beverages (non-alcoholic, excluding water):
- A: -15 to 1 points
- B: 2 to 5 points
- C: 6 to 9 points
- D: 10 to 18 points
- E: 19 points and above

Cheeses:
- A: -15 to -1 points
- B: 0 to 2 points
- C: 3 to 9 points
- D: 10 to 16 points
- E: 17 points and above

Added fats, oils, nuts, seeds, spreads:
- A: -15 to -1 points
- B: 0 to 2 points
- C: 3 to 6 points
- D: 7 to 14 points
- E: 15 points and above

Interpretation:
- 'A' is green and indicates highest nutritional quality.
- 'E' is red and indicates lowest nutritional quality, usually high in sugars, salt, saturated fats, and/or low in fiber and fruit/vegetable content.
